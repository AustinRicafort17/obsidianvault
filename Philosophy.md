PHILOSOPHY READING PATH: BEGINNER → ADVANCED

BEGINNER

1. Sophie’s World — Jostein Gaarder
2. The Problems of Philosophy — Bertrand Russell
3. Think — Simon Blackburn
4. What Does It All Mean? — Thomas Nagel
5. Meditations — Marcus Aurelius

ANCIENT PHILOSOPHY

1. Apology — Plato
2. Euthyphro — Plato
3. Republic — Plato
4. Nicomachean Ethics — Aristotle
5. Meditations — Marcus Aurelius
6. Enchiridion — Epictetus

MEDIEVAL PHILOSOPHY

1. Confessions — Augustine
2. Summa Theologiae — Thomas Aquinas
3. Proslogion — Anselm

EARLY MODERN PHILOSOPHY

1. Meditations on First Philosophy — René Descartes
2. Leviathan — Thomas Hobbes
3. Ethics — Baruch Spinoza
4. An Essay Concerning Human Understanding — John Locke
5. Treatise of Human Nature — David Hume
6. Critique of Pure Reason — Immanuel Kant

19TH CENTURY

1. On Liberty — John Stuart Mill
2. Utilitarianism — John Stuart Mill
3. Thus Spoke Zarathustra — Friedrich Nietzsche
4. Beyond Good and Evil — Friedrich Nietzsche
5. The World as Will and Representation — Arthur Schopenhauer
6. Fear and Trembling — Søren Kierkegaard

20TH CENTURY

1. The Myth of Sisyphus — Albert Camus
2. Being and Nothingness — Jean-Paul Sartre
3. Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus — Ludwig Wittgenstein
4. Philosophical Investigations — Ludwig Wittgenstein
5. The Second Sex — Simone de Beauvoir
6. A Theory of Justice — John Rawls

ADVANCED

1. Critique of Pure Reason — Kant
2. Phenomenology of Spirit — Hegel
3. Being and Time — Martin Heidegger
4. Philosophical Investigations — Wittgenstein
5. Process and Reality — Alfred North Whitehead
6. Truth and Method — Hans-Georg Gadamer

MAIN AREAS TO EXPLORE

Metaphysics — What is reality?  
Epistemology — What can we know?  
Ethics — What should we do?  
Logic — What makes reasoning valid?  
Political Philosophy — How should society be organized?  
Philosophy of Mind — What is consciousness?  
Philosophy of Science — What is science and how does it produce knowledge?  
Existentialism — What does it mean to exist?  
Philosophy of Language — How does language create meaning?  
Aesthetics — What is art and beauty?

SIMPLE PATH

Socrates → Plato → Aristotle → Augustine → Descartes → Hume → Kant → Nietzsche → Wittgenstein → Sartre/Camus → Contemporary Philosophy
